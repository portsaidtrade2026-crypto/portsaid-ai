---
name: PORTSAID logo selection
description: Rationale for choosing the usable uploaded logo variant for storefront branding
---

Use the supplied lossless transparent PNG mark as the primary on-site PORTSAID logo. Keep it on a black backing in light and dark modes, and preserve its aspect ratio rather than forcing it to fill a container.

**Why:** The larger JPEG variants have opaque black margins or include tiny ancillary copy that becomes unreadable in navigation. The transparent PNG stays crisp at typical navigation sizes, while the black backing maintains contrast for the white wordmark on light pages.

**How to apply:** Reuse the primary logo in navigation, checkout and prominent brand placements; avoid scaling it beyond its native resolution or stretching it. If a larger reproduction is needed, revisit the supplied high-resolution originals instead of upscaling the PNG.
