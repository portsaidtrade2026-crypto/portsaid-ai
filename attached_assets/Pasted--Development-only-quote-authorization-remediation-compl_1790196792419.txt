## Development-only quote authorization remediation complete

**Root cause:** The quote detail route checked the authenticated customer’s ownership, but preview, accept, reject, and message operations could act on a quote selected by ID alone. Their customer workflows did not consistently enforce ownership before side effects.

**Rule implemented:** Each of the five quote-ID store routes now selects the quote by **quote ID and customer ID from the trusted authentication context**. A missing or another customer’s quote returns the same non-disclosing 404. Accept, reject, and customer-message workflows also use a shared ownership guard before mutation. Admin routes retain their separate access path. I inspected the other customer-facing quote routes: listing and creation use the authenticated customer ID and do not accept an existing quote ID.

**Boundaries and remaining risk:** During this remediation, no project production app or database was accessed or changed, no production test or migration ran, and nothing was deployed or published. No customer-route quote-ID gap remains in the routes inspected; future quote-ID routes or new internal customer workflow callers must use the same guard. The guard does not transactionally lock a quote against an out-of-band ownership change between authorization and mutation.
